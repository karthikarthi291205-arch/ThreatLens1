Understanding Cyber Threats

Learn about common cybersecurity threats and how they can affect individuals, organizations and systems.

🦠

Malware

Malware is malicious software designed to damage systems, steal information or gain unauthorized access.

Examples

  • Viruses
  • Worms
  • Trojans
  • Spyware
Protection: Keep software updated and use trusted security software.
🎣

Phishing

Phishing is a social engineering technique where attackers attempt to trick users into revealing sensitive information.

Common Targets

  • Passwords
  • Bank information
  • Login credentials
  • Personal information
Protection: Verify links, senders and requests before providing information.
🔒

Ransomware

Ransomware is malicious software that can prevent access to files or systems and may demand payment from victims.

Common Impact

  • File encryption
  • Data loss
  • System disruption
  • Business interruption
Protection: Maintain secure backups and keep systems updated.
🌐

DDoS Attack

A Distributed Denial-of-Service attack attempts to overwhelm a service or network with a large amount of traffic.

Possible Effects

  • Website slowdown
  • Service disruption
  • Network congestion
  • Unavailable services
Protection: Use traffic monitoring, filtering and appropriate network protection services.
🎭

Social Engineering

Social engineering manipulates people into revealing information or performing actions that may compromise security.

Examples

  • Impersonation
  • Pretexting
  • Fake support calls
  • Manipulation
Protection: Verify identities and avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily.
🔑

Password Attacks

Password attacks attempt to obtain or guess passwords to gain unauthorized access to accounts or systems.

Examples

  • Brute force
  • Password spraying
  • Credential stuffing
  • Dictionary attacks
Protection: Use strong unique passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.
