PHISHING AWARENESS

Learn to Identify Phishing

Understand common phishing warning signs and learn how to examine suspicious messages safely.

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a type of social engineering attack in which an attacker attempts to deceive a person into revealing sensitive information or performing an unsafe action.

Phishing attempts may appear through emails, messages, websites, phone calls or other communication methods.

STAY ALERT

Common Warning Signs

⚠️

Urgent Requests

Messages may pressure you to act immediately or claim that your account will be closed.

🔗

Suspicious Links

Links may lead to unexpected websites or domains that do not match the claimed organization.

📧

Unknown Sender

Check the sender's address carefully before trusting an email or message.

🔑

Credential Requests

Be cautious when a message asks for passwords, verification codes or sensitive information.

INTERACTIVE LEARNING

Can You Spot the Warning Signs?

Example Email

From: security@example-alert.com

Subject: Urgent: Verify Your Account

Hello User,

We detected unusual activity on your account. Your account may be restricted unless you verify your information immediately.

Please click the button below to verify your account.

SAFETY TIPS

Think Before You Click

  • Check the sender's address.
  • Hover over links before opening them.
  • Do not share passwords or verification codes.
  • Verify unusual requests through an official channel.
  • Report suspicious messages when appropriate.
